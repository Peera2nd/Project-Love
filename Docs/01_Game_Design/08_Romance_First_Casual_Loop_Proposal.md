Session 040, 2026-10-25. Owner reports that play remains hard to understand and resembles real professional work. The owner now explicitly prioritizes romance/story/characters, with much simpler management secondary. This changes the design priority; the mechanics below are proposals for discussion, not implemented or approved numeric rules. Session 038 remains runtime.

## Diagnosis grounded in owner feedback

The current loop requires understanding queue readiness, dependencies, internal vs external deadlines, pressure, uncertain production, shared-time attribution and Producer duties before an action's value becomes clear. Several controls are already functional, but the player lacks a legible daily objective and reward cycle. More icons/tutorial paragraphs cannot remove this decision burden. Do not generalize one owner's feedback into market/player research evidence.

The experience should center on choosing whom to spend time with and what to do together. Studio work supplies shared goals, situations and romantic opportunities. Cut routine scheduling obligations and hidden failure cascades rather than retaining the entire simulation behind a cleaner screen.

## Proposed core loop

Morning recap -> one visible project/milestone goal and character opportunities -> choose a person/activity -> show immediate outcome preview -> play a short scene -> show actual gains -> advance one phase -> next morning report shows growth/unlocks.

Maintain the 30-day campaign and fixed isometric room/VN presentation. A first trial uses only Days 1–3 of that campaign. Major activities consume a phase; routine talk may retain the existing free-repeat/per-person daily reward policy, with no need to relearn daily optimization. Exact phase/action availability is not newly locked.

Three primary activities:

| Activity | Main purpose | Clear outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Work together | Shared studio experience with a character | Small reliable project contribution plus affection/shared scene |
| Spend personal time/date | Romance, discovery and memories | Stronger relationship/scene/topic opportunity, no extra project contribution required |
| Producer work | Support the studio economy/project | Predictable Money/project gain, no separate mandatory Producer progress ladder |

Rest, gifts and shopping remain supporting actions when useful, without introducing another mandatory daily checklist. Prefer time as the main activity budget in the simplified trial; remove Stamina as a second required optimization/softlock layer initially. Existing Stamina systems can remain in the historical prototype, not as invisible punitive rules in the new loop. Values/actions/resources remain proposals.

**Explicit old-rule revision:** a direct Work Together project reward would replace the Session 034–037 relationship-only/no helper-output rule. A direct Producer project reward and no separate Producer duty meter would revise its current own-work-only semantics. These are proposed changes motivated by the owner's new simplicity goal, not silently applied decisions. If retained, rewards should be bounded/nonstacking; do not reintroduce the old every-department coordination-buff network.

Team baseline work proceeds reliably without user task juggling. Normal play should have ample project progress and romance time without mandatory OT or perfect planning. Routine output/rewards should be predictable; authored events can visibly change the situation and offer understandable responses. Avoid hidden Stress/RNG spirals as the ordinary difficulty source. This is a tuning target, not proven feasibility.

## Preserve planning through characters

Use one weekly milestone and three broad character work cards. Ask for one support focus per week, such as whose part of the demo receives the Producer's attention. This changes relevant opportunities/scenes and a modest visible benefit, without requiring manual start/due edits, every task's acceptance or prerequisite repair. A default team plan functions when the player leaves the board alone.

Dependencies may still explain situations and the order of story/work unlocks, but the team handles ordinary handoffs automatically. Surface a simple working/waiting/done state only when it helps a choice. An art-brief problem becomes a conversation/activity decision with clear consequences, not a graph the player must repair. Do not combine per-worker speed modes, individual deadlines, special queues and more planning currencies into the simplified proposal.

Keep Queue/Timeline as preserved reference prototypes, outside the required romance-first play path. Do not automatically expand scope with an advanced management mode merely to retain prior development effort. Reuse stable clocks, characters, rooms, event arbitration, dialogue, data authoring and transaction/save safety where they fit; retaining every planning formula is not a requirement.

## Feedback, growth and forgiving consequences

Show one main project meter and a readable near-term milestone. Character affection and new topics/memories/scenes communicate relationship growth. Avoid adding a skill tree/level system just to make growth visible. Consider compact mood states instead of three precise Stress counters in the casual path; reducing the permanent work dashboard is a proposed revision to earlier UI requirements, not an implemented change.

Before confirming, show what a major activity costs in time and what it reliably gives. Afterward, show that action's actual reward; baseline team gains belong in a separate short daily summary so causality is honest. Morning recap: project before/after, who became closer, any new scene/topic and today's suggested opportunity. Suggested opportunities guide without forcing a route or assigning all three characters as daily chores.

Personal-time scenes provide more intimacy/character discovery than shared work, so romance does not become a work-efficiency optimization. Affection changes familiarity and access, not automatic compliance or guaranteed identical reactions. Work setbacks can produce different scenes, reduced presentation polish or a recovery opportunity; ordinary scheduling mistakes should not silently close the romance route. Keep meaningful choices/consequences without requiring professional planning skill.

## Next validation before more implementation

Replace Session 039's immediate advanced committed-plan reaction expansion with a lightweight paper/interaction design for the first three days: one visible milestone, clear primary activity previews, a few relationship scenes, one simple work-related dilemma, immediate reward feedback and morning recap. Trial it with the owner before authoring a full new campaign. No new final narrative or math is approved here.

Acceptance questions for actual future trials: can a newcomer identify a next action without a management explanation; can they explain an action's progress/relationship result; can they enjoy a character scene without harming baseline project viability; does the weekly focus create a character choice without mandatory board micromanagement? Then test 30-day time/progress/relationship/recovery across reasonable playstyles. Prior 164 Edit/31 Play passes only validate the current complex prototype, not this new loop.
