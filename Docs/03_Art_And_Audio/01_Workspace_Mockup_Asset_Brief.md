## Agreed presentation

- Action scenes are fixed-view isometric rooms, with HUD access to money, date/time, player Stamina, inventory, options and destination selection. No walking controller or walk animations are required.
- Clicking a character opens conversation/contextual actions. During conversation, dim the scene behind a larger VN character, dialogue and choices. The backdrop remains visible; block background interactions while the modal is open. Dim/fade effects are generated by the UI, not painted dark-background assets.
- Change room-character pose and placement when the phase changes, such as working at a desk in the morning and using a phone on the sofa in the afternoon. Initial transitions may directly swap states; no walking interpolation is required.
- Scene/event hotspots can represent work, rest, a dropped item or a shop clerk. The clerk opens a purchase panel. Inventory/shop/travel are implemented in the room prototype; these images originally scoped their first visual examples.
- User references establish composition/interaction, not a mandatory pixel-art style, final cast or reusable licensed game assets. Copies are retained outside Assets at [room reference](../../Art_Source/Mockup/References/ref_isometric_room.png) and [conversation reference](../../Art_Source/Mockup/References/ref_dialogue_overlay.png).

## Important change from Session 016: local character poses

- Room-character sprites are local transparent cutouts, not full-screen layers fixed to a seat. Use approximately 1024 x 1024 for a consistent local canvas, or another consistent source size if art already exists.
- Keep character scale and isometric camera angle consistent with both rooms. Only draw directions needed by the desk/sofa/counter; no eight-direction set is needed. Furniture positions can be arranged to match the available poses.
- Keep a clear stable base/contact reference near the lower center of the pose canvas. Exact seated offsets/pivots are authored per pose during integration; no Unity configuration is required from the artist. Supply an annotated room composite showing intended desk/sofa/clerk placements.
- Do not pre-cut the torso/legs to one desk silhouette. Keep the usable body pose; independent scene occluders handle furniture in front. Do not bake desk/chair/sofa into the character unless explicitly delivered as a deliberately shared composite, which is not the default.
- Scene placements such as `desk_left` and `sofa_right` and their phase schedules belong in scene/character authoring data, not filenames, baked pixel positions or hardcoded C#.
- Already-prepared full-frame character layers can be kept as working source/reference; export local pose variants for integration. Do not delete existing source art or imported metas to comply with this update.

## Format, naming and placement

- PNG, 8-bit per channel, sRGB. Characters/items/foregrounds need real alpha. Scene exteriors may be transparent or have a flat surround; the supplied room is a 1024x1024 image fitting aspect-preserved in the separate 1920x1080 HUD frame, with a generated black surround.
- Prefixes: `bg_` scene base, `fg_` scene foreground, `chr_` character and `itm_` item. ASCII names, underscores, consistent character/scene keys and stable runtime export names. Phase suffixes: Morning/Evening/Night. Do not append final2/v3 to runtime files.
- Room pose labels (Seated_Working, Seated_Phone, Standing_Normal) are independent of VN CharacterExpression keys. Binding/placement remain data references rather than automatic filename parsing.
- Runtime export root: `D:/Work/Unity/Project_Game/Visual Novel/Project Love/Assets/_Project/Art/`. Prepared folders: Backgrounds/Mockup, Characters/Sprites/Mockup/Room, Characters/Sprites/Mockup/Dialogue and Items/Mockup.
- Optional editable PSD/PSB/KRA masters, original references and annotated composites: `D:/Work/Unity/Project_Game/Visual Novel/Art_Source/Mockup/`, outside Unity Assets. Example `ref_workspace_layout.png` shows intended desk and sofa anchors; `ref_ui_layout.png` shows HUD/modal placement.
- Update imported images in place and keep their `.meta`; move/rename through Unity. Filenames are a handoff convention, not final roster approval.
